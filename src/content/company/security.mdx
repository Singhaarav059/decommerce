Security at Decommerce is built in four layers. Each one assumes the layer above it could fail.

## Access

Staff accounts use role-based permissions. A cashier sees the till, a picker sees the pick list, and only owners see payouts. Every sign-in can require a second factor.

## Encryption

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS and at rest on the storage layer. Card data is handled by certified payment partners and never stored by Decommerce.

## Audit

Price changes, refunds, stock adjustments and permission changes are written to an audit log that store owners can review and export.

## Backups

Store data is backed up on a schedule and restores are tested. Ask us for current retention periods during your demo.
