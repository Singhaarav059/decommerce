### B2B commerce
Selling to other businesses, usually in bulk, with negotiated or tiered prices and credit terms.

### Clienteling
Using a customer's history (past purchases, sizes, preferences) to help them in store.

### Dark store
A small warehouse laid out like a shop but closed to the public, used to fulfil fast local deliveries.

### Endless aisle
Letting a shopper in a store buy an item the store does not have on the shelf, for delivery from another location.

### Marketplace
A third-party sales channel where many sellers list products, such as a large online retailer.

### OMS (order management system)
Software that receives every order and decides which location fulfils it.

### POS (point of sale)
The till: software and hardware used to take payment in a store.

### Quick commerce
Delivery of everyday goods within minutes, usually from a dark store nearby.

### SKU (stock keeping unit)
A code for one exact variant of a product, for example one colour in one size.

### Split shipment
Fulfilling one order from more than one location when no single location has every item.

### TMS (transport management system)
Software that plans delivery routes, assigns vehicles and tracks parcels.

### Unified commerce
Running every sales channel from one system with one catalog, one stock count and one customer record.

### WMS (warehouse management system)
Software that tracks where every item sits in a warehouse and guides picking and packing.
