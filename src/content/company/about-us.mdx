Decommerce is the commerce platform from Demaze Technologies. It joins the storefront, the warehouse, the delivery van and the shop counter into one system, so a product has one price and one stock count wherever it is sold.

Demaze has spent six years building software for businesses. Decignal, our sister company, builds AI applications. Decommerce applies the same engineering to retail and wholesale.
